# AI Enabled Scenario

## Business Objectives
- Help presales teams convert customer discovery notes into structured solution scope faster.
- Improve consistency of PRDs, architecture assumptions, and estimation inputs.

## Functional
- Paste or upload discovery notes, RFP excerpts, and customer emails.
- AI-assisted extraction of requirements, assumptions, risks, and questions.
- Human review and approval before downstream output generation.

## AI Requirements
- Use structured output validation and trace every generated item to source text.
- Separate deterministic calculations from AI recommendations.
- Include evaluation criteria, prompt versioning, safety controls, privacy handling, and feedback monitoring.

## Non-functional
- Local mock mode must work without paid AI services.
- Live AI mode may be enabled only with approved provider credentials.

## Open Items
- Approved model provider, retention policy, and evaluation thresholds are not confirmed.
