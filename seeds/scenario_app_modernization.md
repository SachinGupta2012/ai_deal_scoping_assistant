# App Modernization Scenario

## Business Objectives
- Replace a legacy customer onboarding portal with a responsive browser experience.
- Reduce onboarding drop-off by 30% and shorten support handling time.

## Functional
- Guided onboarding workflow with profile creation and document upload.
- Admin review queue for support staff to resolve exceptions.
- Status notifications for customers.

## Integrations
- Salesforce CRM REST API for customer account updates.
- Approved identity provider for sign-in and role-based access.

## Non-functional
- 25,000 monthly users, P95 onboarding flow under 3 minutes.
- SOC2-aligned audit logging, encryption, and operational monitoring.

## Open Items
- Final identity provider, peak concurrency, and migration volume are not confirmed.
